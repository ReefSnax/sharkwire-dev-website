What SHARKWIRE is

SHARKWIRE is a maker and technical-service label — a single, stable place to host the tools, services, and releases built independently for the web. The projects themselves live elsewhere, under their own names; this page is the fixed point that connects them back to one source.

There is no roadmap on display here and nothing to sign up for. When a project is ready to be public, it is listed. Until then, this page stays exactly this simple.
